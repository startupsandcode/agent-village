01 / The handoff route

Autonomy.
With a clear
line of sight.

One agreed goal. Six deliberate handoffs. Each owner moves the mission forward with something the next person can inspect.

Sequence matters. Design comes first.
  1. 01

    Maya / Agreement

    Make the goal explicit.

    Agree one outcome, acceptance criteria, and the boundaries of the mission.

    Handoff: an agreed brief

  2. 02

    Susie / Design

    Give the goal a shape.

    Supply the design before Sam implements: hierarchy, responsive states, and interaction intent.

    Handoff: the approved design guide

  3. 03

    Sam / Implementation

    Build within the boundary.

    Turn the design into a working page within the agreed scope.

    Handoff: a reviewable build

  4. 04

    Quinn / Behavior

    Check what it actually does.

    Check behavior, including keyboard use, retry limits, reset, and cancellation.

    Handoff: recorded functional findings

  5. 05

    Chromium + Susie / Rendering

    Look at the real result.

    Chromium captures desktop and narrow rendering. Susie then reviews those actual screenshots for hierarchy, spacing, typography, and overflow.

    Handoff: captures and visual findings

  6. 06

    Maya / Verified preview

    Report the proof.

    Report a verified preview only after behavior checks and screenshot review, with evidence and remaining limitations.

    Handoff: a preview worth reviewing

02 / The mission controls

A retry has
a boundary.

Progress needs a stopping rule.
Try the policy in three attempts.

Ready. This illustration allows two retries after an initial failed check.

Retries used: 0 of 2.

[ 02 ]

Bounded, by design.

Bounded retries cap repeated attempts: one initial check, then at most two retries. At the limit, Maya must review the outcome before work continues.

Cancellation ends the current example. Reset starts a fresh demonstration.

What a preview means

A preview is a temporary, reviewable build. It is not a production release, merge, or proof that every possible scenario works.

These controls are an illustration. They do not run agents or deploy anything. Verification requires recorded behavior checks plus desktop and narrow screenshot review.
